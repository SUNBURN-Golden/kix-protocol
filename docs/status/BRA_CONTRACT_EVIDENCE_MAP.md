## 1. Vocabulary

- `COVERED`: sufficient existing deterministic evidence for the scoped mock or
  local FSM predicate. It does not establish external/production properties.
- `PARTIAL`: cited local evidence exists, but a named part of the clause remains
  untested or outside the suite. No missing semantics are invented.
- `NOT_COVERED`: no test evidence establishes the named predicate; evidence is
  `none`. An explicit false flag is not evidence of real currentness or routing.
- `OPEN`: adopted mock boundary with `UNDETERMINED` inputs and named contract
  owners. It is not a request to implement an alternative or fill a policy value.

Owner `none` assigns no owner. Every other owner/route is contract-verbatim;
multiple routes are separated by semicolons. The aggregate §7.1/§7.2 owner
`사용자` does not replace the item-specific owners below. `§5 잠금` is the
contract's storage lock route, not an invented owning person.

or judge their assertions' semantic adequacy. It is **local only**, not wired

same-module TestCase subclasses in `test_adopt_issued_atomicity.py`. The checker

  local state/journal remain unchanged. General atomicity across machines is not
  established. `test_char_adopt_issued_admitted_ticket_is_not_issued` is instead
  class A reachability of the ISSUED guard.

  retained snapshots beyond specified immutable first-result replay.
- The restore-fingerprint tests check contract-defined live normalization and
  restored state equality, while post-restore retry-response equivalence is
  explicitly contract undefined. They do not settle all future retry semantics.
